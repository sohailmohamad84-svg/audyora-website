Data Governance & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Standards

At Audyora ("Audyora", "we", "our", or "us"), we maintain the highest standards of data governance, security, and confidentiality in our B2B pipeline architecture, outbound lead generation, and demand marketing operations.

1. Scope of B2B Data Collection

We collect and process professional business contact data solely for legitimate B2B commercial interest purposes. This includes business names, professional email addresses, corporate telephone numbers, job titles, and publicly disclosed organizational data. We do not collect or store personal sensitive data.

2. GDPR, CCPA & CAN-SPAM Compliance

Our outbound and content syndication methodologies strictly comply with:

  • EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Processing under legitimate interest for relevant business communications, with transparent opt-out rights.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): Protection of consumer identity with immediate access, disclosure, and deletion procedures.
  • CAN-SPAM Act: Clear sender identification, transparent physical address disclosure, and instantaneous one-click unsubscribe processing.

3. Data Retention and Security

All campaign communications and inquiry briefs submitted via our web forms are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and stored in hardened, SOC-2 compliant database environments.

4. Your Rights & Opt-Out Requests

Any professional contact has the unconditional right to request removal from our databases, request access to stored data, or decline further communication. To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@audyora.com.
